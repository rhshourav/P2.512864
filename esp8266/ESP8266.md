

## Pinout for testing P2.512864
```
  PANEL PIN (HUB75E)        ESP8266 PIN
  ==================        ===========
       [ R1 ] ------------> D5 (GPIO14)
       [ G1 ] ------------> D7 (GPIO13)
       [ B1 ] ------------> D6 (GPIO12)
       [GND ] ------------> GND
       [ R2 ] ------------> (Leave Disconnected)
       [ G2 ] ------------> (Leave Disconnected)
       [ B2 ] ------------> (Leave Disconnected)
       [  E ] ------------> GND (Wire directly to Ground)
       [  A ] ------------> D0 (GPIO16)
       [  B ] ------------> D1 (GPIO5)
       [  C ] ------------> D2 (GPIO4)
       [  D ] ------------> D3 (GPIO0)
       [CLK ] ------------> D4 (GPIO2)
       [LAT ] ------------> D8 (GPIO15)
       [ OE ] ------------> RX (GPIO3)  *Remember to unplug this when uploading code!
       [GND ] ------------> GND
```

##  Two Important Reminders for This Test:

* 1. The 'E' Pin Override: Because we are forcing a 1/16 scan trick on a 1/32 panel, connecting the panel's E pin directly to GND grounds the 5th address line. This tricks the panel into displaying data on the top section.
* 2. Ground Sharing: Make sure the GND from your external 5V power supply, the panel's GND pins, and the ESP8266's GND pin are all wired together into a single common connection. If they aren't shared, the data signals will glitch out completely.
